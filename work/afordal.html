Afordal

A real estate platform for buyers, sellers, agents, and loan officers, taken from two unfinished wireframes to a shipped, live product, since reused as the foundation for a separate commercial real estate joint venture.

The ask

A stakeholder had a rough idea and a set of wireframes that were three years old and never finished. No user flows, no content strategy, no design system.

No spec, no research, no existing patterns, no stakeholder clarity on who the product was really for. My job was to figure out what this product actually needed to be before I could design a single real screen.

The real problem

Afordal wasn’t one user with one journey. Buyers, sellers, agents, and loan officers all crossed paths, each arriving under different circumstances. A buyer might already have an agent, or might not. A seller might be working with a loan officer, or might not need one yet.

On top of that, IDX and MLS compliance rules put hard limits on what listing data I could show and how it could be displayed and attributed outside the source system.

I couldn’t design a single screen until I understood how these people and these rules intersected. The hard part wasn’t the UI, it was untangling who the product was for and what rules it had to obey.

The system

Because there was no existing system, I built one from scratch: a component library, type and color rules, and a brand asset library. This wasn’t just for the buyer/seller app. It’s the same system that carried into the agent and loan officer CRM and the marketing site, which meant every new screen after this point got faster and more consistent to design and build.

The ecosystem

Afordal isn’t a single app. It’s four connected pieces built on one design system, which meant a consistent brand and interaction language across every audience, without separate design efforts for each piece.

Buyer/seller app

Home value stats, an AI-assisted dashboard steering buyers and sellers toward the right product offerings, linked back to the agent’s IDX home search.

CRM

For agents and loan officers to manage their leads and clients. Loan officers show up branded to the specific agent or brokerage they’re connected to.

Marketing site

Brings agents and loan officers onto the platform in the first place.

IDX home search

Shareable search agents send to their own contacts, single or multiple MLS, with the agent positioned as the largest brand on the page. Reskin of an existing HomeASAP tool, worth being upfront about, but shows the reach of the system.

Outcome

Afordal shipped as a solo six-month build, covering brand, design system, product, and marketing site, and is live today.

Separately, that same UI foundation was reused as the basis for a commercial real estate SaaS product through a joint venture, a downstream application beyond the original consumer-facing platform, and proof the system was built to extend past its first use case.
