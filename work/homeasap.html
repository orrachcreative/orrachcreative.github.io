The ask

Take a real estate product, give it a brand, then build the marketing site that sells it, checkout included. Then do it again. And again. HomeASAP had a full lineup of real estate products [exact count needed], and I got to give each one its own identity and its own place to buy it.

The real problem

The hard part wasn’t any single site. It was the volume. Every product meant its own brand, its own site, its own checkout flow, all running on WordPress, sharing nothing between them to start.

The system

Underneath all of it sat a design system for the HomeASAP brand, the thing that let every product site feel like it came from the same company even with its own identity layered on top. I led a team of three, myself included, and together we built the corporate site that tied it all together: 20-plus pages.

{% include media-placeholder.html label="System/component overview" tall=true %}

The ecosystem

The product sites weren’t standalone. They connected back through a Facebook app called Real Estate Agent Directory, which tied the whole lineup together.

Outcome

Conversions went up on the individual product sites once the FAQ change shipped. And once the team moved to one shared theme with a shared component library, building and improving new sites got faster and more consistent, for the sites already live and every one built after.

That’s the part I’m proudest of, it kept paying off long after the project was done.
