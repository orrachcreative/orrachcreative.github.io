The ask

The brief was a phone call. No deck, no written spec, just someone talking through what they wanted.

I had full control over the design, but no one to bounce ideas off. Nobody was pushing back or challenging a direction, so every call was mine to make, and mine alone to catch if it was wrong.

The real problem

Afordal had to work for two different people: agents and loan officers, each wanting different things out of the same system.

Agents needed a CRM for their leads and clients, a consumer-facing dashboard where buyers and sellers could see their home's value and get AI-assisted product recommendations tied back to the agent's IDX home search, and an IDX search that put the agent forward as the biggest brand on the page.
